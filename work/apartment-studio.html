Apartment Walkthrough Studio

Turns a floor plan into a 3D apartment you can walk through first-person.

Press V to walk, E to customize a surface. On a phone, tap First-Person Mode.

About 16 MB to download. Phones get the lighter quality setting, about 7 MB.

Problem

A floor plan shows where the walls are, not what the place feels like. Choosing finishes and placing furniture from a flat drawing is guesswork.

What I did

I built a studio that turns a floor plan into a 3D apartment. The demo is The Lakeview One-Bedroom. You walk it first-person, or switch to a dollhouse view to move furniture.

You can swap finishes, upload photos onto surfaces, set the room lights, and export a PDF design book.

How it works

The floor plan is a layout file. The studio builds the rooms and furniture from it, then renders them in the browser.
