About

The person who automates the spreadsheet, then goes looking for the next spreadsheet.

Profile

Barrington, Illinois. Open to relocating, with Texas at the top of the list.

I am a Staff Accountant at Ortho Molecular Products, a $200M multi-entity manufacturer, where I own month-end deliverables across two legal entities on a five-day close. That is the day job, and I do it properly: journal entries, reconciliations, fixed assets, sales tax, the books of a subsidiary brand.

The other half of the role is why I am here. I came in with a Computer Science degree from the University of Pittsburgh, and within months the VP of Accounting had me leading the team's automation work. Since then I have shipped four production systems, including an OpenAI-backed transaction classifier that cut a 36-hour monthly review to under ten minutes, fixed a Fast Close report at the SQL level after months of bad data, and been pulled across the org to write the specification layer for a company-wide platform consolidation.

The pattern in all of it: I sit at the seam between the people who understand the finance and the people who build the systems, and I am comfortable on both sides of it. I sit for the CPA exams in October 2026. Off the clock I build and ship products, and I am learning quantum computing in Qiskit, honestly framed as study, not expertise.
